# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. What Safeguarding Means
Safeguarding is about:

- Protecting students from harm.

- Preventing abuse, exploitation and neglect.

- Supporting wellbeing and mental health.

- Making sure concerns can be raised and acted upon.

Safeguarding is everyone's responsibility, not just staff responsibility. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCD1D89BD-C062-4463-A0F3-C6E87565FC37%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. College Is Different from School
At college:

- You have more independence.

- You make more of your own decisions.

- Staff monitor less directly.

- You can self-refer for support.

- Staff still act when there are concerns.

You do not need to wait for a teacher to notice a problem. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCD1D89BD-C062-4463-A0F3-C6E87565FC37%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. What Prevent Is
Prevent helps protect people from being drawn into:

- Radicalisation.

- Extremism.

- Terrorism.

Extremism includes opposition to fundamental British Values or encouragement of violence against others. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCD1D89BD-C062-4463-A0F3-C6E87565FC37%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Vulnerability Factors
People may become more vulnerable when they are:

- Isolated.

- Looking for belonging.

- Struggling with identity.

- Angry or frustrated.

- Influenced by manipulative online content.

- Influenced by people encouraging harmful views. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCD1D89BD-C062-4463-A0F3-C6E87565FC37%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Online Safety Risks
Key risks include:
#### Personal Information
Information shared online can be misused.
#### Grooming and Exploitation
People may build trust online for harmful purposes.
#### Financial Scams
Fake jobs, fake offers and "easy money" schemes often target young people.
#### Non-Consensual Images
Sharing intimate or private images without consent is unlawful.
#### Dark Web Risks
The dark web carries significant legal and personal risks. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCD1D89BD-C062-4463-A0F3-C6E87565FC37%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. AI and Academic Integrity
Do NOT:

- Submit AI-generated work as your own.

- Use AI content without acknowledgement.

- Enter personal or confidential information into public AI tools.

Do:

- Declare AI use when required.

- Use AI to support learning, not replace learning.

- Check AI-generated information.

- Ask your teacher if unsure. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCD1D89BD-C062-4463-A0F3-C6E87565FC37%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. AI and Data Protection
Never enter into public AI systems:

- Personal details.

- Student information.

- Staff details.

- Confidential college information.

If you would not share it publicly, do not enter it into an AI tool. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCD1D89BD-C062-4463-A0F3-C6E87565FC37%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. How ESCG Responds to Concerns
The safeguarding process is:

1. A concern is raised.

2. The safeguarding team is informed.

3. The concern is assessed.

4. Appropriate support or action follows.

Staff cannot promise complete secrecy if there is a risk of harm. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCD1D89BD-C062-4463-A0F3-C6E87565FC37%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. How to Report Concerns
If worried about yourself or someone else:

- Tell a trusted member of staff.

- Contact the safeguarding team.

- Contact the wellbeing team.

- Report anonymously if needed.

You do not need proof to report a concern. # YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. The Biggest Year 2 Risk Is Complacency
Students already know the systems.

The risk is:

- Assuming problems are fine.

- Not reporting concerns.

- Becoming casual about safety.

- Thinking support is no longer needed.

"Don't fumble the last lap." [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BE2A3E4F3-AF4E-4A1C-A896-D5E09503294F%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. Year 2 Pressures Are Different
Common Year 2 pressures include:

- Final Major Projects.

- UCAS and progression applications.

- Industry placements.

- Financial pressures.

- Relationships.

- Freelance work.

These pressures can increase safeguarding risks. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BE2A3E4F3-AF4E-4A1C-A896-D5E09503294F%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Safeguarding Still Matters
Nothing has changed about:

- Access to safeguarding support.

- Access to wellbeing services.

- Reporting routes.

- Staff responsibilities.

Many Year 2 students need support but use it less often. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BE2A3E4F3-AF4E-4A1C-A896-D5E09503294F%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Peer Responsibility Becomes More Important
By Year 2:

- You know your peers.

- You recognise changes in behaviour.

- You spot concerns earlier.

That knowledge is a responsibility.

If someone appears to be struggling, encourage them to seek support or raise a concern. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BE2A3E4F3-AF4E-4A1C-A896-D5E09503294F%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. New Online Risks
Year 2 brings:

- Job scams.

- Fake freelance opportunities.

- Fake agency offers.

- Exploitative contracts.

- Greater public visibility through portfolios.

Professional identity becomes more important. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BE2A3E4F3-AF4E-4A1C-A896-D5E09503294F%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. AI Rules Matter More
Year 2 assessed work receives greater scrutiny.

Examples include:

- Final Major Projects.

- Occupational Specialism Projects.

- Major coursework.

- Externally moderated assessments.

Students must understand and follow AI declaration requirements. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BE2A3E4F3-AF4E-4A1C-A896-D5E09503294F%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Consent Matters
A key Year 2 reminder:

Always obtain proper consent when:

- Filming people.

- Photographing people.

- Sharing media.

Familiarity is not permission. Friends still deserve consent. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BE2A3E4F3-AF4E-4A1C-A896-D5E09503294F%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Reporting Concerns Is Still Essential
Many Year 2 students avoid reporting concerns because they think they should manage independently.

This is a mistake.

The correct action is still:

- Tell someone early.

- Seek support.

- Use safeguarding and wellbeing routes. [[Module F -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BE2A3E4F3-AF4E-4A1C-A896-D5E09503294F%7D&file=Module%20F%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. Professional Reputation Matters
Your digital footprint now affects:

- University applications.

- Placement opportunities.

- Employment prospects.

- Professional reputation.

Your online presence is part of your professional identity.
