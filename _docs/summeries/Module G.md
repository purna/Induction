# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Mental Health Is Health
Mental health exists on a spectrum:

- Thriving

- Coping

- Struggling

- In Crisis

Everyone moves along this spectrum throughout the year.

Mental health is affected by:

- Sleep

- Money

- People

- Workload

- Home life

- Physical activity

Struggling is information, not weakness. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA755826F-C324-461A-9DFB-D3A732CB42D9%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. Recognising Stress
Signs of stress can include:

- Trouble starting work.

- Trouble stopping work.

- Changes in sleep.

- Irritability.

- Avoidance.

- Falling behind with coursework or portfolios.

Common causes include:

- Deadlines.

- Workload.

- Financial pressures.

- Home pressures.

- Balancing study and work. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA755826F-C324-461A-9DFB-D3A732CB42D9%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. What Actually Helps Stress
Evidence-based strategies include:
#### Move
Regular physical activity, even walking.
#### Connect
Spending time with people.
#### Sleep
Protecting healthy sleep habits.
#### Talk
Speaking to somebody about what is happening. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA755826F-C324-461A-9DFB-D3A732CB42D9%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Sleep Is a Study Tool
Sleep affects:

- Memory.

- Concentration.

- Focus.

- Emotional regulation.

Good sleep habits include:

- Consistent wake-up times.

- Limiting screen use before bed.

- Reducing caffeine later in the day.

Revision and learning depend on sleep for memory consolidation. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA755826F-C324-461A-9DFB-D3A732CB42D9%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Money Can Affect Wellbeing
Financial stress can affect:

- Mood.

- Sleep.

- Attendance.

- Concentration.

- Motivation.

Students should know that support such as hardship funding exists and that asking for help is normal. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA755826F-C324-461A-9DFB-D3A732CB42D9%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Support Routes Exist
The support process is:

1. Tell someone.

2. Referral is made.

3. Appropriate support is arranged.

You can approach:

- Tutors.

- Teachers.

- Wellbeing staff.

- Support services.

Any trusted adult can be the starting point. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA755826F-C324-461A-9DFB-D3A732CB42D9%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Tell College What It Needs to Know
Inform the college about:

- Disabilities.

- Learning needs.

- Mental health conditions.

- Circumstances affecting study.

Support can only be arranged when the college is aware of the need. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA755826F-C324-461A-9DFB-D3A732CB42D9%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Look Out For Others
The process is simple:
### Notice
Changes in behaviour.
### Ask
"Are you actually okay?"
### Tell
A trusted adult if serious support is needed.

Helping someone get support is not betrayal. # YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Year 2 Is Heavier
Common Year 2 pressures include:

- Final Major Projects.

- Employer Set Projects.

- Coursework.

- Industry placements.

- Progression plans.

- Future career decisions.

The workload is expected to feel heavier than Year 1. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BFB2FA8ED-C624-4D76-BD44-F5B3965D8577%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. Mental Health Still Moves
The same wellbeing spectrum applies:

- Thriving

- Coping

- Struggling

- In Crisis

The difference is that Year 2 students have already seen how their wellbeing changes under pressure.

Recognising your own warning signs is important. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BFB2FA8ED-C624-4D76-BD44-F5B3965D8577%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Stress Often Looks Like Avoidance
Examples include:

- Avoiding Final Major Project work.

- Delaying difficult tasks.

- Avoiding production sessions.

- Ignoring skill gaps.

- Putting off important decisions.

Stress becomes more harmful when it remains switched on continuously. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BFB2FA8ED-C624-4D76-BD44-F5B3965D8577%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. The Same Four Solutions Still Work
The evidence remains the same:

- Move.

- Connect.

- Sleep.

- Talk.

These are maintenance habits, not personality traits. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BFB2FA8ED-C624-4D76-BD44-F5B3965D8577%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Sleep Matters More Than Students Think
A tired brain:

- Learns less effectively.

- Makes poorer decisions.

- Finds projects harder.

- Experiences stronger emotions.

Sleep directly supports final-year success. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BFB2FA8ED-C624-4D76-BD44-F5B3965D8577%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. The Year 2 Support Paradox
A common problem:

The year students most need support is often the year they stop using it.

Many Year 2 students feel they should already be able to cope independently.

This is often the wrong assumption. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BFB2FA8ED-C624-4D76-BD44-F5B3965D8577%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Tell College About Changes
If anything has changed since Year 1, such as:

- Health.

- Home circumstances.

- Mental health.

- Learning support needs.

Tell the college early so support can be adjusted. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BFB2FA8ED-C624-4D76-BD44-F5B3965D8577%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Peer Responsibility Matters More
Year 2 students know each other better.

You are often the first person to notice:

- Withdrawal.

- Missed sessions.

- Changes in behaviour.

- Falling behind.

- Isolation.

Notice, Ask, Tell remains the correct approach. [[Module G -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BFB2FA8ED-C624-4D76-BD44-F5B3965D8577%7D&file=Module%20G%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. Strong Finishes Need Early Support
Students who seek support early are more likely to:

- Complete projects successfully.

- Address technical gaps.

- Manage workload effectively.

- Meet deadlines.

Waiting until the end of the year often creates avoidable problems.
