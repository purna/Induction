# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Health & Safety Starts with Noticing
Most accidents do not happen because someone deliberately breaks the rules.

They happen because people stop noticing:

- Hazards become "normal".

- People are rushing.

- People are distracted.

- People do not want to make a fuss.

Good safety starts by paying attention to your surroundings. [[Module C L...vel 3 Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B884D2D10-7E33-494C-B5D9-96A030E904A0%7D&file=Module%20C%20Level%203%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 2. What Switches Noticing Off
Common causes include:

- Rushing.

- Thinking "it's always been like that."

- Phones and other distractions.

- Not wanting to speak up.

Good habits include:

- Asking "Is this normal?"

- Saying concerns out loud.

- Treating near misses as learning opportunities. [[Module C L...vel 3 Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B884D2D10-7E33-494C-B5D9-96A030E904A0%7D&file=Module%20C%20Level%203%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 3. Safety Basics Everyone Must Know
You should know:

- Your fire exit route.

- Your assembly point.

- Who to tell if something is wrong.

- That walkways and fire exits must stay clear.

- PPE rules must always be followed when required. [[Module C L...vel 3 Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B884D2D10-7E33-494C-B5D9-96A030E904A0%7D&file=Module%20C%20Level%203%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 4. Reporting Problems Is Part of Safety
Tell your teacher if you notice:

- Hazards.

- Near misses.

- Damaged equipment.

- Facilities issues.

- Something unsafe.

Reporting is not making a fuss. It is helping keep everyone safe. [[Module C L...vel 3 Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B884D2D10-7E33-494C-B5D9-96A030E904A0%7D&file=Module%20C%20Level%203%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 5. Show Respect
Health and safety is linked to the PROUD value of Show Respect.

Respect means:
#### For the Space
- Tidy up.

- Report damage.

- Leave things as you found them.
#### For Other People
- Do not interfere with their equipment.

- Do not create unnecessary risks.
#### For Staff
- Respect safety decisions and instructions. [[Module C L...vel 3 Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B884D2D10-7E33-494C-B5D9-96A030E904A0%7D&file=Module%20C%20Level%203%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 6. Media & Studio Safety
Important subject-specific rules include:

- Closed-toe shoes in studios.

- Use sandbags on lighting stands.

- Use camera wrist straps.

- Secure tripods correctly.

- Store cables properly.

- Complete risk assessments before location shoots.

- Return equipment after use.

These are industry-standard expectations. [[Module C L...vel 3 Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B884D2D10-7E33-494C-B5D9-96A030E904A0%7D&file=Module%20C%20Level%203%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 7. Responsibility Is Shared
Your responsibilities include:

- Using equipment safely.

- Returning equipment.

- Reporting faults.

- Following procedures.

College responsibilities include:

- Equipment testing.

- Building maintenance.

- Electrical safety.

- Health and safety systems. [[Module C L...vel 3 Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B884D2D10-7E33-494C-B5D9-96A030E904A0%7D&file=Module%20C%20Level%203%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 8. If Something Feels Unsafe
Safety is not only about physical hazards.

You should speak up if:

- A space feels unsafe.

- An activity feels unsafe.

- Someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable.

Tell someone early. [[Module C L...vel 3 Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B884D2D10-7E33-494C-B5D9-96A030E904A0%7D&file=Module%20C%20Level%203%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

# YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Familiarity Is the Biggest Risk
In Year 1 everything felt new.

In Year 2:

- You become comfortable.

- You assume things are fine.

- Habits become automatic.

- Hazards can stop standing out.

Familiarity can lead to shortcuts and missed risks. [[Module C L...l 3 Year 2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B833B90C0-17E7-47D5-85FE-6E0DFDDC38DC%7D&file=Module%20C%20Level%203%20Year%202.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 2. Common Year 2 Slips
Examples include:

- Not wearing lanyards consistently.

- Skipping familiar safety checks.

- Assuming someone else checked equipment.

- Ignoring things that would have been reported in Year 1.

- Taking shortcuts with risk assessments. ### 3. Refresh the Basics
Even in Year 2 you must still remember:

- Fire exits.

- Assembly points.

- Reporting routes.

- PPE requirements.

- Safe working practices.

- Respect equipment.

- Respect safety instructions.

Depending on your pathway, this may include:

- Broadcasting responsibilities.

- Editorial duties.

- Industry placements.

- More independent work.

These activities bring additional professional responsibilities. ### 6. Risk Assessments Still Matter
A common mistake is saying:

"We've filmed there before."

This is not a valid reason to skip a risk assessment.

Every location shoot still requires appropriate approval and planning. ### 7. Equipment Responsibility
Year 2 students must:

- Return equipment correctly.

- Report faults.

- Store equipment properly.

- Avoid assuming someone else has done it.

If you are the last user, responsibility is yours. The core message remains:

- Notice hazards.

- Notice unsafe behaviour.

- Notice uncomfortable situations.

- Tell someone early.
