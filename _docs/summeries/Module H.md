# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Why Navigate Matters
Navigate helps you:

- Track your development.

- Build a Digital CV.

- Record evidence of skills and achievements.

- Prepare for university, apprenticeships and employment.

Your Digital CV becomes evidence of what you can actually do, not just what you claim you can do. [[Module H -...Level Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC41AD3DC-8760-48F9-90D5-A34FF87AB44D%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. Own Your Progress
At Level 3:

- You are responsible for your development.

- You must track skills honestly.

- You need evidence for future applications.

- Independent study is expected.

Navigate helps you manage that responsibility. [[Module H -...Level Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC41AD3DC-8760-48F9-90D5-A34FF87AB44D%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Skills Assessment
The Skills Assessment is a self-evaluation tool.

The results are:
### Green
A proven strength.
### Amber
Inconsistent skill that needs improvement.
### Red
A priority area for development.

The most useful improvements often come from focusing on Amber areas. [[Module H -...Level Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC41AD3DC-8760-48F9-90D5-A34FF87AB44D%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Be Honest
A Skills Assessment only works if you answer honestly.

Overestimating your ability gives a misleading picture and reduces its value. [[Module H -...Level Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC41AD3DC-8760-48F9-90D5-A34FF87AB44D%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Plan Your Destination
You should identify:

- University goals.

- Degree pathways.

- Apprenticeships.

- Employment routes.

A goal without entry requirements or a plan is only an idea. [[Module H -...Level Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC41AD3DC-8760-48F9-90D5-A34FF87AB44D%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Research Careers Properly
When investigating careers, consider:

- Entry routes.

- Qualification requirements.

- Day-to-day responsibilities.

- Salary and progression.

Eliminating unsuitable careers is also useful progress. [[Module H -...Level Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC41AD3DC-8760-48F9-90D5-A34FF87AB44D%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Log Activities Regularly
Useful things to record include:

- Projects.

- Coursework.

- Creative work.

- Coding work.

- Industry visits.

- Placements.

- Part-time employment.

- Work experience.

Small, regular updates prevent large catch-up workloads later. [[Module H -...Level Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC41AD3DC-8760-48F9-90D5-A34FF87AB44D%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Reflection Matters
A strong reflection explains:

- What happened.

- What decisions were made.

- Why those decisions were made.

- What evidence supports them.

- What will change next time.

Reflection is more than a description of events. [[Module H -...Level Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC41AD3DC-8760-48F9-90D5-A34FF87AB44D%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. Complete the First Two Weeks Checklist
You should:

1. Set up Navigate.

2. Complete the Skills Assessment.

3. Interpret results.

4. Set development priorities.

5. Record a destination goal.

6. Complete the career quiz.

7. Write a Digital CV introduction.

8. Write and submit at least one reflection. [[Module H -...Level Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC41AD3DC-8760-48F9-90D5-A34FF87AB44D%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

# YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. The Purpose of Navigate Is More Important Now
By Year 2:

- Applications are approaching.

- Major assessments are taking place.

- Evidence matters more.

Your Digital CV should now be demonstrating real achievements and growth. ### 2. Your Skills Assessment Should Drive Development
Use the assessment to:

- Identify weaknesses.

- Set priorities.

- Track progress.

- Gather evidence of improvement.

Amber areas often provide the best opportunities for growth. [[Module H -...Level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B4FD92027-1331-448E-A81E-F7DD2E3BEE6B%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Your Destination Must Be Specific
You should know:

- Where you want to go next.

- Relevant entry requirements.

- Qualification expectations.

- Deadlines and timelines.

Vague ambitions become problems in Year 2. [[Module H -...Level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B4FD92027-1331-448E-A81E-F7DD2E3BEE6B%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Career Investigation Must Be Realistic
Research careers carefully:

- Daily responsibilities.

- Working conditions.

- Progression routes.

- Required skills.

Discovering that a career is not for you is valuable information. [[Module H -...Level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B4FD92027-1331-448E-A81E-F7DD2E3BEE6B%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Your Digital CV Should Contain Evidence
Evidence might include:

- Final projects.

- Industry placements.

- Professional work.

- Creative productions.

- Technical projects.

- External competitions.

- Industry visits.

- Part-time jobs.

Evidence should demonstrate skills and development. [[Module H -...Level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B4FD92027-1331-448E-A81E-F7DD2E3BEE6B%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Reflection Is a Professional Skill
Strong reflection:

- Analyses decisions.

- Identifies causes.

- Evaluates outcomes.

- Explains improvements.

Professional portfolios require analytical reflection rather than simple description. [[Module H -...Level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B4FD92027-1331-448E-A81E-F7DD2E3BEE6B%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Logging Activities Saves Time Later
Regular updates create:

- Better portfolios.

- Better personal statements.

- Better applications.

- Better evidence of employability.

Ten minutes now can save hours later. [[Module H -...Level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B4FD92027-1331-448E-A81E-F7DD2E3BEE6B%7D&file=Module%20H%20-%20Level%203%20and%20A%20Level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Independence Is Expected
Year 2 students are expected to:

- Manage deadlines.

- Complete Navigate independently.

- Ask for help early when needed.

- Track progress consistently.

Professional behaviour includes requesting support before problems become major barriers.
