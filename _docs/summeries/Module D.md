# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. What is Students' Voice?
Students' Voice is the process through which students influence:

- Teaching and learning.

- Student experience.

- College facilities.

- Equipment and software.

- Timetabling and support. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B66B3C116-0C98-4A9C-AEF2-2E612675D891%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

Good student feedback can lead to real improvements when it is specific and evidence-based. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B66B3C116-0C98-4A9C-AEF2-2E612675D891%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. What Makes Students' Voice Effective?
Students' Voice works best when:

- Reps consult their group.

- Feedback is specific.

- Students raise issues before meetings.

- People follow up on agreed actions.

Example:

✅ "Four edit stations cannot run Premiere."

❌ "The software is rubbish." [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B66B3C116-0C98-4A9C-AEF2-2E612675D891%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Ways to Get Involved
There are four main ways:
#### Student Representative
Represents your class and attends Student Council meetings.
#### Student Governor
Represents students at Governing Board level.
#### NUS Membership
Provides access to student benefits and discounts.
#### Surveys and Forums
Give students another way to influence college decisions. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B66B3C116-0C98-4A9C-AEF2-2E612675D891%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. What Does a Student Rep Do?
A Student Rep:

- Gathers student opinions.

- Attends Student Council meetings.

- Gives feedback about teaching and facilities.

- Helps find solutions.

- Reports back to the group.

- Represents both the course group and English/Maths class. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B66B3C116-0C98-4A9C-AEF2-2E612675D891%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Why Become a Student Rep?
The role develops:

- Communication skills.

- Leadership skills.

- Problem-solving.

- Professional conduct.

- Employability skills.

These are qualities employers value. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B66B3C116-0C98-4A9C-AEF2-2E612675D891%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. What is a Student Governor?
Student Governors:

- Sit on the Main Governing Board.

- Contribute to strategic decisions.

- Help shape budgets and resources.

- Bring a student perspective to leadership discussions.

Governors are not the same as Representatives.

Reps represent a specific group.

Governors contribute to decisions affecting the whole college. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B66B3C116-0C98-4A9C-AEF2-2E612675D891%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Encourage Unity
The featured PROUD value is:
### Encourage Unity
This means:

- Working together.

- Sharing concerns collectively.

- Supporting representatives.

- Improving things for everyone, not just yourself.

Evidence from a group has more influence than a single complaint. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B66B3C116-0C98-4A9C-AEF2-2E612675D891%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Elections
Each group elects:

1. A Course Representative.

2. An English/Maths Representative.

Representatives must:

- Be elected fairly.

- Be registered officially.

- Attend required training. # YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Year 2 Students Have Experience
Year 2 students have:

- Already experienced Students' Voice.

- Seen good and poor representation.

- Gained knowledge about how the college works.

This experience makes their feedback more valuable. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B9F8C01F3-DB9F-439F-B566-405E42DE2E4E%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. What Makes Students' Voice Work?
Students' Voice works when:

- Reps communicate with students.

- Feedback is evidence-based.

- Students engage with the process.

- Outcomes are followed up.

Student Voice is a shared responsibility, not just the rep's responsibility. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B9F8C01F3-DB9F-439F-B566-405E42DE2E4E%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Familiarity Is Both an Advantage and a Risk
Advantages:

- You know staff.

- You know systems.

- You understand how decisions happen.

Risks:

- Going through the motions.

- Not challenging issues.

- Becoming disengaged.

Good Year 2 reps use experience actively. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B9F8C01F3-DB9F-439F-B566-405E42DE2E4E%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Student Rep Responsibilities
Student Reps:

- Gather feedback.

- Attend three Student Council meetings.

- Represent class views.

- Help develop solutions.

- Keep students informed.

- Represent English/Maths as well as the main course. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B9F8C01F3-DB9F-439F-B566-405E42DE2E4E%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Student Governor Responsibilities
Student Governors:

- Help shape strategic decisions.

- Provide student perspectives.

- Challenge and question decisions.

- Act in the interests of the whole college.

Governors do not represent individual classes or subjects. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B9F8C01F3-DB9F-439F-B566-405E42DE2E4E%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. What the Board Does
The Governing Board is responsible for:

- Strategic planning.

- College quality.

- Finances and resources.

- Student experience.

- Holding senior leaders accountable. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B9F8C01F3-DB9F-439F-B566-405E42DE2E4E%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Student Governor Commitment
The role involves:

- Around 3 to 5 hours per month.

- Board meetings.

- Committee meetings.

- Reading reports.

- Training and preparation.

The role requires reliability and professionalism. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B9F8C01F3-DB9F-439F-B566-405E42DE2E4E%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Encourage Unity in Year 2
Year 2 students should:

- Share what they have learned.

- Support newer students.

- Use experience to improve the course.

- Help future cohorts benefit from improvements.

The next year's students inherit the systems and standards that current students leave behind. [[Module D -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B9F8C01F3-DB9F-439F-B566-405E42DE2E4E%7D&file=Module%20D%20-%20A%20Level%20and%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. Use Your Voice
Ways to contribute include:

- Voting.

- Briefing your rep.

- Standing for election.

- Applying to be a Governor (if eligible).

- Completing surveys.

- Raising concerns early.
