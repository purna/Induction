# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. The Shift from School to College
College is different because:

- Nobody chases missing work. Responsibility is yours.

- You have more unstructured time and must manage it yourself.

- Problems often appear weeks or months after the original mistake.

- Independence is expected and developed through practice. [[Module B Level 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BD6CB7977-3906-4A57-8C65-1FF6197B366F%7D&file=Module%20B%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 2. The "Long Fuse" Principle
A missed task today rarely causes an immediate problem.

Instead:

- Missing brief reviews, lessons or coursework now creates gaps later.

- Catching up becomes harder when new work is arriving at the same time.

- Planning free periods in advance helps prevent this. [[Module B Level 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BD6CB7977-3906-4A57-8C65-1FF6197B366F%7D&file=Module%20B%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 3. Seek Opportunity
One of ESCG's PROUD values.

This means:

- Join clubs and activities.

- Volunteer or take part in opportunities.

- Explore careers and progression routes.

- Build skills outside lessons.

- Treat mistakes as learning opportunities. [[Module B Level 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BD6CB7977-3906-4A57-8C65-1FF6197B366F%7D&file=Module%20B%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 4. Confidence
Confidence is not a personality trait.

Real confidence comes from:

- Doing difficult things.

- Coping with challenges.

- Asking for help when needed.

- Understanding that feeling out of depth is normal. [[Module B Level 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BD6CB7977-3906-4A57-8C65-1FF6197B366F%7D&file=Module%20B%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 5. Attendance Matters Because...
You miss more than slides when absent:

- Teacher explanations.

- Worked examples.

- Class discussions.

- Relationships with staff and classmates.

- Opportunities and strong references. [[Module B Level 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BD6CB7977-3906-4A57-8C65-1FF6197B366F%7D&file=Module%20B%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 6. Attendance Barriers Are Real
Barriers can include:

- Mental health difficulties.

- Financial or travel issues.

- Family pressures.

- Health conditions.

- Lack of support for neurodivergence.

- Feeling isolated or not fitting in.

The important action is to tell someone early so support can be provided. [[Module B Level 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BD6CB7977-3906-4A57-8C65-1FF6197B366F%7D&file=Module%20B%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 7. One Habit Can Change Outcomes
Successful change usually starts with one specific action, such as:

- Planning a free period.

- Asking for help.

- Taking a Seek Opportunity action.

- Talking about a barrier to attendance. [[Module B Level 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BD6CB7977-3906-4A57-8C65-1FF6197B366F%7D&file=Module%20B%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

# YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Year 2 Is Different
Year 1 was about building foundations.

Year 2 is about:

- Final assessments.

- Portfolios.

- Final Major Projects (FMP).

- Industry placements.

- Preparing progression routes. [[Module B C...L-3-YEAR-2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B8D1EC027-7B9C-45A4-A2EE-20533BA9A28D%7D&file=Module%20B%20Confidence%20LEVEL-3-YEAR-2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 2. Gaps Compound
Any weaknesses carried from Year 1 become bigger.

Examples:

- Missing skills.

- Incomplete portfolio work.

- Poor organisation.

- Lower attendance.

Year 2 gives much less room for error. [[Module B C...L-3-YEAR-2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B8D1EC027-7B9C-45A4-A2EE-20533BA9A28D%7D&file=Module%20B%20Confidence%20LEVEL-3-YEAR-2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 3. The Final Fuse
Missed work hurts most near assessment deadlines.

Students often struggle because they are:

- Catching up on old work.

- Producing new work at the same time.

The most effective strategy remains planning time in advance and using free periods productively. [[Module B C...L-3-YEAR-2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B8D1EC027-7B9C-45A4-A2EE-20533BA9A28D%7D&file=Module%20B%20Confidence%20LEVEL-3-YEAR-2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 4. Seek Opportunity in Year 2
Progression should become active.

Examples:

- Applying for university.

- Applying for apprenticeships.

- Building a portfolio.

- Completing placement evidence.

- Publishing work online.

- Developing industry experience. [[Module B C...L-3-YEAR-2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B8D1EC027-7B9C-45A4-A2EE-20533BA9A28D%7D&file=Module%20B%20Confidence%20LEVEL-3-YEAR-2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 5. The Year 2 Confidence Trap
#### Trap 1: Coasting
"Year 1 was fine; I don't need to change anything."
#### Trap 2: The Wall
Struggling but not asking for help.

The solution to both:

- recognise the issue early,

- ask for support early. [[Module B C...L-3-YEAR-2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B8D1EC027-7B9C-45A4-A2EE-20533BA9A28D%7D&file=Module%20B%20Confidence%20LEVEL-3-YEAR-2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 6. Attendance Matters More in Year 2
Attendance supports:

- Final assessment success.

- Understanding grade boundaries.

- Staff references.

- Industry and university applications.

- Visibility of your effort and development. [[Module B C...L-3-YEAR-2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B8D1EC027-7B9C-45A4-A2EE-20533BA9A28D%7D&file=Module%20B%20Confidence%20LEVEL-3-YEAR-2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 7. Barriers Still Need Support
Common barriers include:

- Burnout.

- Pressure and stress.

- Financial difficulties.

- Family responsibilities.

- Health needs.

- Neurodivergence.

The key message is the same: tell someone early. [[Module B C...L-3-YEAR-2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B8D1EC027-7B9C-45A4-A2EE-20533BA9A28D%7D&file=Module%20B%20Confidence%20LEVEL-3-YEAR-2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
### 8. Focus on One Habit
A single practical action is more useful than multiple vague plans.

Examples:

- Use one free period effectively.

- Resolve unfinished Year 1 work.

- Take a progression step.

- Discuss a barrier with staff. [[Module B C...L-3-YEAR-2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B8D1EC027-7B9C-45A4-A2EE-20533BA9A28D%7D&file=Module%20B%20Confidence%20LEVEL-3-YEAR-2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)
