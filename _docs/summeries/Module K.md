# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. This Course Opens Multiple Routes
Level 3 qualifications can lead to:

- University.

- Degree apprenticeships.

- Apprenticeships.

- Skilled employment.

- Further study.

There is no single "correct" route. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B78B67BA4-54D1-441B-AAED-C392F8CA4699%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. University Is Not the Only Option
Possible progression routes include:

- Media Production degrees.

- Film degrees.

- Computing degrees.

- Cybersecurity degrees.

- Animation and Games degrees.

- Photography and Design degrees.

Degree apprenticeships and employment are equally valid routes. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B78B67BA4-54D1-441B-AAED-C392F8CA4699%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Navigate Builds Evidence
Navigate contains:

- Your Digital CV.

- Skills Assessment results.

- Opportunities record.

- Placement evidence.

- Project evidence.

Keeping Navigate updated makes future applications significantly easier. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B78B67BA4-54D1-441B-AAED-C392F8CA4699%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Evidence Beats Memory
Applications and references are stronger when they include:

- Dated evidence.

- Project records.

- Placement records.

- Skills development.

If an achievement is not recorded, it becomes harder to use later. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B78B67BA4-54D1-441B-AAED-C392F8CA4699%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Aspirations and Goals Are Different
### Aspiration
"I want to work in games."
### Goal
"By half-term I will have produced one portfolio piece suitable for a games application."

Goals include:

- Specific outcomes.

- A deadline.

- An action.

- A first step. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B78B67BA4-54D1-441B-AAED-C392F8CA4699%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Think in Three Horizons
#### This Term
What can you achieve soon?
#### This Year
What should be achieved by summer?
#### On Completion
Where do you want to go after college?

Strong planning links all three horizons together. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B78B67BA4-54D1-441B-AAED-C392F8CA4699%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Course Doubts Are Normal
There are two kinds of uncertainty:
### The Course Is Hard
Usually solved through support and adaptation.
### The Course Is Wrong
May require discussions about changing direction.

The earlier concerns are raised, the easier they are to address. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B78B67BA4-54D1-441B-AAED-C392F8CA4699%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Careers Support Exists
Students can seek help from:

- Careers teams.

- Personal tutors.

- Heads of Curriculum.

Career planning should not be done alone. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B78B67BA4-54D1-441B-AAED-C392F8CA4699%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. Progression Starts Now
Future applications are built from:

- The work completed this term.

- Portfolio development.

- Skills evidence.

- Placement experiences.

The first term matters more than many students realise. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B78B67BA4-54D1-441B-AAED-C392F8CA4699%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

# YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. The Application Cycle Is Live
Year 2 is no longer about exploring possibilities.

It is about:

- Making applications.

- Meeting deadlines.

- Using evidence.

- Making decisions.

Progression is now active, not theoretical. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B0E14C85F-404A-4D20-8B7F-628D98B219E8%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. Dates Matter
Major deadlines include:

- UCAS applications.

- Apprenticeship applications.

- Internal reference deadlines.

- Predicted grade deadlines.

The key message is:

Early beats on-time. On-time is the new late. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B0E14C85F-404A-4D20-8B7F-628D98B219E8%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Navigate Becomes Application Material
Your Navigate record should now supply:

- UCAS content.

- Apprenticeship evidence.

- Portfolio evidence.

- References.

- Interview preparation.

Year 2 is about using the evidence collected in Year 1. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B0E14C85F-404A-4D20-8B7F-628D98B219E8%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Audit Your Record
Ask yourself:

- Is my Digital CV current?

- Are projects fully logged?

- Are placements recorded?

- Is my skills evidence visible?

Gaps become harder to fix later. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B0E14C85F-404A-4D20-8B7F-628D98B219E8%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Goals Must Have Margin
Year 2 goals require:

- Specific deadlines.

- Early completion targets.

- Clear first steps.

Goals should be designed to beat deadlines rather than merely meet them. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B0E14C85F-404A-4D20-8B7F-628D98B219E8%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Work Backwards From Results Day
Planning should start with:
### Results Day
Then work backwards through:
### January Deadlines
Then:
### Current-Term Actions
This ensures the important outcome stays visible. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B0E14C85F-404A-4D20-8B7F-628D98B219E8%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. References Matter More Than Ever
Year 2 behaviour influences:

- References.

- Applications.

- Offers.

- Interviews.

Professional standards remain essential. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B0E14C85F-404A-4D20-8B7F-628D98B219E8%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. It Is Still Possible to Change Direction
Year 2 conversations often involve:
### The Route Feels Shaky
Support and clarification may help.
### The Route Is Wrong
Course or destination changes may still be possible.

Delaying these conversations reduces available options. [[Module K -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B0E14C85F-404A-4D20-8B7F-628D98B219E8%7D&file=Module%20K%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. The Last Lap Matters Most
Year 2 is about:

- Applications.

- Assessment.

- Portfolios.

- Placements.

All of these happen in parallel.

Planning early protects the time needed for achieving strong grades. # Quick Revision Summary
### Year 1
- Explore all progression routes.

- Build evidence in Navigate.

- Learn the difference between aspirations and goals.

- Use the three-horizon planning model.

- Seek careers advice early.

- Start building a future-focused portfolio.
### Year 2
- Applications are now active.

- Audit your evidence regularly.

- Work backwards from deadlines.

- Build goals with clear dates and margin.

- Use support when needed.

- Don't fumble the final year: applications, portfolio work and grades all matter simultaneously.
