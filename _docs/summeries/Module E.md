# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Respect Looks Different in Different Settings
Respect applies:

- In college.

- On industry placement.

- Online.

- In future workplaces.

College expects students to increasingly self-regulate and behave professionally. [[Module E -...el3 Year 1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B04E5A900-FA05-4202-8CF3-9889C133177E%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level3%20Year%201.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. The PROUD Values
These are the core ESCG values:
### P – Bring Positivity
Create a welcoming and supportive environment.
### R – Show Respect
Treat everyone fairly and appropriately.
### O – Seek Opportunity
Take opportunities to learn and develop.
### U – Encourage Unity
Work positively with others and challenge exclusion.
### D – Celebrate Diversity
Value differences and recognise that diversity strengthens the community. [[Module E -...el3 Year 1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B04E5A900-FA05-4202-8CF3-9889C133177E%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level3%20Year%201.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. British Values
The four British Values are:
### Democracy
Students can influence college through elections, surveys and feedback.
### Rule of Law
College rules and UK law apply to everyone.
### Individual Liberty
People can make their own choices, provided they do not harm others.
### Mutual Respect and Tolerance
Everyone deserves fair treatment regardless of background. [[Module E -...el3 Year 1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B04E5A900-FA05-4202-8CF3-9889C133177E%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level3%20Year%201.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. The Equality Act 2010
The Equality Act protects people from discrimination based on nine protected characteristics:

1. Age

2. Disability

3. Gender reassignment

4. Marriage and civil partnership

5. Pregnancy and maternity

6. Race

7. Religion or belief

8. Sex

9. Sexual orientation [[Module E -...el3 Year 1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B04E5A900-FA05-4202-8CF3-9889C133177E%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level3%20Year%201.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Important Equality Act Terms
#### Discrimination
Treating someone unfairly because of a protected characteristic.
#### Harassment
Unwanted behaviour that creates a hostile or offensive environment.
#### Victimisation
Treating someone unfairly because they raised or supported a concern about discrimination. [[Module E -...el3 Year 1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B04E5A900-FA05-4202-8CF3-9889C133177E%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level3%20Year%201.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Behaviour Standards
The same standards apply:

- In college.

- On placement.

- Online.

Key expectations:

- Respectful language.

- No bullying or harassment.

- No discriminatory behaviour.

- Responsible use of technology.

- No recording or sharing content without permission.

- Professional conduct on placements. ### 7. Your Digital Footprint Matters
Remember:

- Online content can be permanent.

- Employers may see your online activity.

- Social media behaviour can have college consequences.

- College policies can apply to online conduct. [[Module E -...el3 Year 1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B04E5A900-FA05-4202-8CF3-9889C133177E%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level3%20Year%201.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Zero Tolerance
ESCG has zero tolerance for:

- Harassment.

- Discrimination.

- Bullying.

- Abuse.

Serious consequences can include:

- Formal disciplinary procedures.

- Suspension.

- Permanent exclusion. [[Module E -...el3 Year 1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B04E5A900-FA05-4202-8CF3-9889C133177E%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level3%20Year%201.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. How to Report Concerns
There are three reporting routes:

1. Tell your teacher.

2. Use the formal reporting system.

3. Use anonymous reporting.

All concerns are taken seriously. # YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Year 2 Is About Application
Year 2 is not about learning these ideas for the first time.

It is about:

- Applying them consistently.

- Challenging poor behaviour.

- Taking responsibility for standards.

- Acting professionally in familiar environments. [[Module E -...l 3 Year 2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA69ABD82-C052-44B4-9D71-A27FE7E1BAC9%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level%203%20Year%202.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. Familiarity Creates Risk
When people know each other well:

- Poor behaviour can become normalised.

- Inappropriate jokes may be accepted.

- Exclusion may be overlooked.

- Standards can slip.

Familiarity is not an excuse for poor conduct. [[Module E -...l 3 Year 2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA69ABD82-C052-44B4-9D71-A27FE7E1BAC9%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level%203%20Year%202.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. PROUD Values in Year 2
Year 2 students should:

- Set the standard for others.

- Include people who may be left out.

- Maintain professionalism.

- Help develop and support others.

Being a role model matters more in Year 2. [[Module E -...l 3 Year 2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA69ABD82-C052-44B4-9D71-A27FE7E1BAC9%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level%203%20Year%202.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. British Values Require Action
By Year 2 you already know the values.

The challenge is to:

- Use your voice.

- Challenge inappropriate behaviour.

- Respect rules.

- Show tolerance and respect under peer pressure. [[Module E -...l 3 Year 2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA69ABD82-C052-44B4-9D71-A27FE7E1BAC9%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level%203%20Year%202.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Equality Act Responsibilities
The Equality Act is not only about your rights.

You also have responsibilities:

- Do not discriminate.

- Do not harass others.

- Do not victimise others.

- Do not ignore behaviour that targets others.

Knowing the law and applying it are different things. [[Module E -...l 3 Year 2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA69ABD82-C052-44B4-9D71-A27FE7E1BAC9%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level%203%20Year%202.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Behaviour Standards Remain the Same
This applies:

- In college.

- On placement.

- Online.

In Year 2:

- People notice your professionalism.

- Placement references matter.

- Your conduct affects progression opportunities. [[Module E -...l 3 Year 2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA69ABD82-C052-44B4-9D71-A27FE7E1BAC9%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level%203%20Year%202.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Audit Your Online Presence
You should:

- Review old posts.

- Consider what employers might see.

- Remove inappropriate content where possible.

- Maintain professional standards online. [[Module E -...l 3 Year 2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA69ABD82-C052-44B4-9D71-A27FE7E1BAC9%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level%203%20Year%202.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Zero Tolerance Includes Bystanders
A major Year 2 message:

Allowing harmful behaviour to continue can make you part of the problem.

Students should:

- Challenge concerns.

- Report concerns.

- Support affected people.

- Avoid remaining silent when action is needed. [[Module E -...l 3 Year 2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BA69ABD82-C052-44B4-9D71-A27FE7E1BAC9%7D&file=Module%20E%20-%20Level%203%20Year%202.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. Reporting Concerns Is a Responsibility
Reporting is not simply an option.

Do not assume:

- Someone else has reported it.

- Someone else will deal with it.

Use:

1. Teacher reporting.

2. Formal reporting systems.

3. Anonymous reporting routes.
