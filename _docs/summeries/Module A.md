# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Belonging Matters
One of the biggest factors in student success is feeling that you belong.

College is easier when:

- You know people in your group.

- You feel comfortable asking questions.

- You have staff you can approach.

- You build positive connections early. [[Module A -...Level Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCA9614FC-87C0-487E-83F7-6779E45ED336%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20L3_%20T%20Level%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. Your Teachers Are Here to Help
You can expect staff to:

- Know your name.

- Treat you with respect.

- Teach you effectively.

- Help when you are stuck.

In return, staff expect students to:

- Attend.

- Participate.

- Treat others respectfully.

- Communicate early when problems arise. [[Module A -...Level Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCA9614FC-87C0-487E-83F7-6779E45ED336%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20L3_%20T%20Level%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Your Study Programme Has Several Parts
A study programme includes:
#### Your Main Qualification
Coursework, assessments, portfolios and projects.
#### English and Maths
If still required.
#### Work-Related Learning
Placements, employer projects and industry experiences.
#### Tutorials and Personal Development
Support for progress, wellbeing and future planning. [[Module A -...Level Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCA9614FC-87C0-487E-83F7-6779E45ED336%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20L3_%20T%20Level%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Learn to Use Pro Portal
Pro Portal is your main college information system.

You use it to:

- Access your timetable.

- Check room changes.

- Read messages.

- View attendance.

- Track progress.

Checking it daily is strongly recommended. [[Module A -...Level Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCA9614FC-87C0-487E-83F7-6779E45ED336%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20L3_%20T%20Level%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. It Is Normal to Have Worries
Common worries include:

- Workload.

- New software.

- Meeting people.

- Confidence.

- Finding your way around.

Most students share similar concerns when they start. [[Module A -...Level Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCA9614FC-87C0-487E-83F7-6779E45ED336%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20L3_%20T%20Level%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Support Exists
College support may include:

- Learning support.

- Mental health and wellbeing support.

- Financial support.

- Careers advice.

Seeking help early is a sensible decision, not a sign of weakness. [[Module A -...Level Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCA9614FC-87C0-487E-83F7-6779E45ED336%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20L3_%20T%20Level%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. College Is More Than Lessons
College life can include:

- Clubs.

- Societies.

- Trips.

- Competitions.

- Enrichment activities.

These experiences can improve confidence, skills and belonging. [[Module A -...Level Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCA9614FC-87C0-487E-83F7-6779E45ED336%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20L3_%20T%20Level%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Reflection Matters
Students should think about:

- What they are looking forward to.

- What support they may need.

- Who they have connected with.

Small reflections help tutors support students effectively. [[Module A -...Level Yr1_ | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BCA9614FC-87C0-487E-83F7-6779E45ED336%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20L3_%20T%20Level%20Yr1_.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

# YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Year 2 Is Different
Year 2 is not about learning how college works.

It is about:

- Completing the course strongly.

- Building your portfolio.

- Preparing for progression.

- Taking the next step into work, university or apprenticeships. [[Module A -...level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC4FE26F0-C7D2-447A-9FF9-1F60C1127884%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20Level%203_%20T%20level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. Know Where You Are Heading
Students should have a developing idea of:

- Employment.

- Apprenticeships.

- University.

- Further study.

It is acceptable not to know yet, but you should start exploring your options actively. [[Module A -...level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC4FE26F0-C7D2-447A-9FF9-1F60C1127884%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20Level%203_%20T%20level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Year 2 Demands More
Key changes include:

- Higher stakes.

- Bigger projects.

- More deadlines.

- Applications.

- Greater independence.

Grades, portfolios and placements now carry significant weight. [[Module A -...level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC4FE26F0-C7D2-447A-9FF9-1F60C1127884%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20Level%203_%20T%20level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. The Biggest Risk Is Drift
Many students do not underperform because they lack ability.

Problems often come from:

- Missing deadlines.

- Reduced attendance.

- Delayed applications.

- Poor organisation.

The key message is:

Don't fumble the last lap. [[Module A -...level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC4FE26F0-C7D2-447A-9FF9-1F60C1127884%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20Level%203_%20T%20level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Stay Organised
Use:

- Pro Portal.

- Calendars.

- Deadline trackers.

Year 2 students should know where all major deadlines are recorded and monitored. [[Module A -...level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC4FE26F0-C7D2-447A-9FF9-1F60C1127884%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20Level%203_%20T%20level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Support Still Matters
Support remains available for:

- Careers.

- Applications.

- Progression.

- Wellbeing.

- Financial issues.

- Academic concerns.

Many Year 2 students stop asking for help when they need it most. [[Module A -...level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC4FE26F0-C7D2-447A-9FF9-1F60C1127884%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20Level%203_%20T%20level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. All Progression Routes Are Valuable
Three major destination routes include:
#### Employment
A strong and valid outcome.
#### Apprenticeships
Earn while learning.
#### University/Higher Education
Degree-based progression.

There is no "second-best" route. [[Module A -...level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC4FE26F0-C7D2-447A-9FF9-1F60C1127884%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20Level%203_%20T%20level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Use a Simple Career Test
Ask:

"Will this give me something meaningful to discuss in an interview?"

Activities that strengthen portfolios, applications and experience are usually worth doing. [[Module A -...level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC4FE26F0-C7D2-447A-9FF9-1F60C1127884%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20Level%203_%20T%20level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. Define Your Final-Year Focus
Students should identify:

- Where they want to be at the end of Year 2.

- One habit that will help them get there.

- One issue they need to address early.

Writing goals increases the likelihood of achieving them. [[Module A -...level Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BC4FE26F0-C7D2-447A-9FF9-1F60C1127884%7D&file=Module%20A%20-%20Level%203_%20T%20level%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

# Quick Revision Summary
### Year 1
- Get settled and build belonging.

- Learn how college systems work.

- Understand your study programme.

- Use Pro Portal regularly.

- Know where to access support.

- Get involved beyond lessons.
### Year 2
- Focus on finishing strongly.

- Decide where you are heading next.

- Avoid drift and missed opportunities.

- Stay organised.

- Continue using support.

- Build the portfolio, experience and evidence needed for your next step.
