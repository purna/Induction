# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Work Experience Is Part of Your Course
Work experience is not an optional extra.

It helps you:

- Build your portfolio.

- Gain real workplace experience.

- Collect references.

- Strengthen applications.

- Test possible career paths. [[Module J -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6714F537-F4CA-4F60-A8B5-E80C5AC07753%7D&file=Module%20J%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. Why Work Experience Matters
Work experience supports:
#### Employability
Provides evidence employers can trust.
#### Progression
Supports applications for university, apprenticeships and jobs.
#### Professional Identity
Helps you discover which areas of the industry suit you best. [[Module J -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6714F537-F4CA-4F60-A8B5-E80C5AC07753%7D&file=Module%20J%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Work Experience Can Take Different Forms
Options may include:

- Block placements.

- Employer visits.

- Volunteering.

- Live project briefs.

- Industry partnerships.

Not every option suits every course. [[Module J -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6714F537-F4CA-4F60-A8B5-E80C5AC07753%7D&file=Module%20J%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. If You Cannot Secure a Placement
You can still take part through:

- Employer-set projects.

- Employer talks.

- Industry activities delivered in college.

Skills Week is never wasted. [[Module J -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6714F537-F4CA-4F60-A8B5-E80C5AC07753%7D&file=Module%20J%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Key Dates
Important dates:

- **Skills Week:** 8 March 2027

- **Placement Request Deadline:** 5 February 2027

Placement requests must be submitted early because employers require checks including:

- Health and safety checks.

- Insurance checks.

- Safeguarding checks. [[Module J -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6714F537-F4CA-4F60-A8B5-E80C5AC07753%7D&file=Module%20J%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Using Navigate
Before requesting a placement you need:

- Company name and address.

- Employer contact details.

- Dates and times.

- Tutor details.

- Placement role information.

Navigate is accessed through:

- My Student Life.

- login.navigate.uk.com. [[Module J -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6714F537-F4CA-4F60-A8B5-E80C5AC07753%7D&file=Module%20J%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Help Is Available
If you need support:

- Contact Careers & Employability.

- Speak to your tutor.

- Ask for help early if a placement is not progressing.

Support is available before and during placement. [[Module J -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6714F537-F4CA-4F60-A8B5-E80C5AC07753%7D&file=Module%20J%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Expectations During Placement
You should:

- Be punctual.

- Follow dress codes.

- Communicate professionally.

- Complete placement journals.

- Log hours accurately.

- Raise concerns quickly.

Professional standards matter from day one. ### 9. Placement Evidence Matters
Employers value:

- References.

- Workplace experience.

- Demonstrated professionalism.

A placement creates both experience and evidence. # YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. This Is Round Two
Year 2 work experience is different because:

- You already have experience.

- You can target your specialism.

- Applications are approaching.

- You can build on Year 1 evidence.

The goal is to go further than last year.
