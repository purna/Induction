# YEAR 1
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Professional Behaviour Applies Everywhere
The same standards apply:

- In college.

- On placement.

- In employment.

Professional behaviour includes:

- Being on time.

- Being prepared.

- Communicating early about problems.

- Treating people respectfully.

- Taking responsibility for mistakes and fixing them. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6E9D5B94-740A-4B62-BFE4-4BBDF918F027%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. Professionalism Is About Habits
Professionalism is not something you switch on for interviews.

It is demonstrated through:

- Reliability.

- Communication.

- Respect.

- Consistency.

- Accountability.

These habits influence how others see you. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6E9D5B94-740A-4B62-BFE4-4BBDF918F027%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. Your Reference Starts Now
Staff references are built from:

- Attendance.

- Punctuality.

- Reliability.

- Professional conduct.

References focus on patterns over time, not isolated mistakes. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6E9D5B94-740A-4B62-BFE4-4BBDF918F027%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Human Skills Matter
Employers often hire based on more than qualifications.

Key employability skills include:
### Communication
Explaining work clearly.
### Teamwork
Working effectively with others.
### Reliability
Doing what you promised to do.
### Problem Solving
Finding solutions independently.
### Adaptability
Handling change well.
### Initiative
Taking action without being asked. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6E9D5B94-740A-4B62-BFE4-4BBDF918F027%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Skills Assessment Results
The Skills Assessment helps identify:

- Strengths.

- Development areas.

- Priorities for improvement.

You should:

- Review your results carefully.

- Compare them with how others see you.

- Choose areas to develop. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6E9D5B94-740A-4B62-BFE4-4BBDF918F027%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Claims Need Evidence
Weak statement:

"I'm a good team player."

Strong statement:

"I organised project deadlines for my group and helped the team submit on time."

The strongest applications include evidence rather than claims. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6E9D5B94-740A-4B62-BFE4-4BBDF918F027%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. A Good Strength Statement Includes:
### Situation
What happened?
### Action
What did you actually do?
### Outcome
What does it demonstrate?

Evidence can come from:

- College projects.

- Placements.

- Part-time jobs.

- Sports.

- Caring responsibilities.

- Volunteering. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr1 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B6E9D5B94-740A-4B62-BFE4-4BBDF918F027%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr1.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Navigate Supports Employability
Navigate should contain:

- Skills assessment results.

- Strength statements.

- Digital CV information.

- Development plans.

This information will support later applications and placements. # YEAR 2
## What You Need to Know
### 1. Year 2 Is About Using Evidence
In Year 1 you built evidence.

In Year 2 you use it for:

- University applications.

- Apprenticeships.

- Placements.

- Employment.

- Interviews.

Applications require examples, not opinions. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BEF70F447-AC39-4FD5-8725-781AD6F842A2%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 2. The Standard Has Not Changed
The same expectations remain:

- Punctuality.

- Reliability.

- Professional communication.

- Respect.

- Accountability.

The difference is that referees and employers are now paying closer attention. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BEF70F447-AC39-4FD5-8725-781AD6F842A2%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 3. References Are Now Active
Year 2 references are:

- Being requested.

- Being written.

- Being read.

Recent behaviour often has the greatest influence on current references. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BEF70F447-AC39-4FD5-8725-781AD6F842A2%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 4. Human Skills Become Interview Currency
Interview questions often assess:

- Communication.

- Teamwork.

- Reliability.

- Problem solving.

- Adaptability.

- Initiative.

Your placement experiences provide valuable evidence for these. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BEF70F447-AC39-4FD5-8725-781AD6F842A2%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 5. Look for Skill Movement
The key Year 2 question is:

What has improved since Year 1?

Employers and universities want evidence of growth as well as achievement. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BEF70F447-AC39-4FD5-8725-781AD6F842A2%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 6. Evidence Matters More Than Ever
Weak application:

"I am reliable."

Strong application:

"I solved a file-access problem during placement and produced guidance for future users."

Evidence creates stronger applications and stronger interviews. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BEF70F447-AC39-4FD5-8725-781AD6F842A2%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 7. Create Application-Ready Strength Statements
The recommended structure is:
### Situation
What happened?
### Action
What you did.
### Result
What it demonstrates.

Strong statements are:

- Specific.

- Verifiable.

- Memorable.

- Evidence-based. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BEF70F447-AC39-4FD5-8725-781AD6F842A2%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 8. Placement Evidence Is Valuable
Year 2 students should prioritise evidence from:

- Industry placements.

- Final projects.

- Professional work.

- Real clients.

- Significant responsibilities.

This evidence is often stronger than classroom examples. [[Module I -...evel 3 Yr2 | PowerPoint]](https://eastsussexcollegegroup-my.sharepoint.com/personal/nigel_morris_escg_ac_uk/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7BEF70F447-AC39-4FD5-8725-781AD6F842A2%7D&file=Module%20I%20-%20Level%203%20Yr2.pptx&action=edit&mobileredirect=true)

### 9. Independence Matters
Professional independence includes:

- Identifying development priorities.

- Acting on feedback.

- Updating Navigate.

- Seeking support early.

- Maintaining standards consistently.

Asking for help remains a professional skill.
